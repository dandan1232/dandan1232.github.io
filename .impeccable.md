## Design Context

### Users
The site serves recruiters, technical peers, and the author as a long-term reference. The notes area helps readers quickly scan engineering topics, open a specific write-up, and read long-form technical material without visual interference.

### Brand Personality
Technical, warm, and imaginative. The experience should feel like a crafted personal portfolio rather than a generic documentation template: capable and precise in the foreground, quietly cinematic in the background.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the existing warm paper/orange identity and dark reading theme. Use the low-poly “Migration / 候鸟” landscape as an ambient full-viewport layer across the notes index and article pages. The artwork is supporting atmosphere, not a second interface: no duplicated controls, labels, poetry, audio, or fullscreen behavior.

### Design Principles

1. Reading always wins: maintain strong text contrast and soften the scene behind content.
2. Preserve the source artwork’s character while removing interactions that compete with the notes.
3. Theme changes should feel environmental: warm paper by day, night flight in dark mode.
4. Motion is progressive enhancement: respect reduced-motion preferences and pause unnecessary work.
5. One shared implementation must cover every note page consistently.
