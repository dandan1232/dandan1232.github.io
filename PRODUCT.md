# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, technical peers, and the author use the site to quickly understand Echo Lin's full-stack and AI engineering work, browse project case studies, and read technical notes.

## Product Purpose

Present a memorable, credible personal portfolio that demonstrates engineering depth through real projects, technical writing, and crafted interactive storytelling. Success means visitors can recognize the author's capabilities quickly and remember the experience afterward.

## Brand Personality

Warm, technical, and imaginative. The experience should feel precise and capable in the foreground, playful and quietly cinematic in motion, and personal rather than templated.

## Anti-references

- Generic documentation templates or interchangeable AI-generated portfolio layouts.
- Cartoon decoration that competes with project content or weakens professional credibility.
- Photorealistic or glossy 3D assets that clash with the established low-poly toy world.
- Duplicate controls, labels, audio, or ornamental UI that distracts from reading.

## Design Principles

1. Show engineering personality through working interactions, not decorative claims.
2. Keep the warm paper, orange, and deep-blue visual identity recognizable across every scene.
3. Let the 3D world create delight while content and navigation remain immediately legible.
4. Preserve one coherent character and motion system across the hero, about, projects, and contact scenes.
5. Prefer crafted, specific details over generic polish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong text contrast, keyboard-usable navigation, meaningful focus states, and responsive layouts. Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid relying on color or animation alone to communicate essential information.
